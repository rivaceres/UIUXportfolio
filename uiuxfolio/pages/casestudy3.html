ProjectReady

A client onboarding platform designed to simplify how businesses and clients prepare for new projects. It brings project requirements, document submissions, task management, and approvals into one centralized workspace. Through a milestone-based journey, clients can track their progress, understand their responsibilities, and complete onboarding with greater clarity and confidence, regardless of the project's nature or complexity.

Problem Statement

Client onboarding often involves multiple tasks, document requests, project requirements, and approvals. When these activities are managed through scattered emails, shared files, and disconnected communication channels, clients may struggle to understand what needs to be completed, what has already been submitted, and what happens next. This lack of structure can lead to confusion, unnecessary follow-ups, and delays before a project can begin.

Target Users

Primary Users

Direct Clients

Individuals or business owners who initiate projects and are responsible for providing requirements, submitting documents, and approving project details.

Client Representatives

Authorized employees or representatives who manage onboarding tasks, coordinate submissions, and approve requirements on behalf of their organization.

Goal

The goal is to create a guided and intuitive onboarding experience that helps clients complete their responsibilities with minimal confusion. By organizing requirements into manageable milestones, centralizing project information, and providing clear progress indicators, ProjectReady aims to simplify the process, improve transparency, and reduce unnecessary waiting between tasks.

Impact

This project proposes a more organized and approachable onboarding experience that could reduce administrative friction, improve communication, and help clients complete requirements more confidently. For businesses and project providers, it could support more consistent information gathering, clearer visibility into pending submissions, and smoother project preparation. These are expected benefits of the proposed design rather than measured outcomes.

Research & Key Insights

ProjectReady was designed as a self-initiated design concept using scenario-based workflow analysis and assumptions about common client onboarding challenges. The exploration focused on understanding how clients receive instructions, submit required information, monitor progress, and respond to feedback. This highlighted the importance of clear task guidance, centralized information, visible progress, and allowing clients to continue their onboarding activities while earlier submissions are under review. These assumptions would require validation through future user research and usability testing.

Process

Ideation

The project starts with understanding the client's onboarding journey and translating it into a structured, easy-to-follow experience. From mapping user interactions to exploring interface layouts, each stage helped shape a more intuitive onboarding process.

The information architecture organizes the platform into four main sections: Dashboard, Tasks & Approvals, Project Brief, and Documents. This structure keeps essential features accessible while simplifying navigation.

The user flows outline how clients complete onboarding tasks, submit requirements, and respond to revisions. The process allows clients to move forward after submission without waiting for provider review, while keeping previously completed milestones accessible.

I then translated the key user journeys into low-fidelity wireframes to explore the initial layout, content structure, and navigation.

The initial layouts are refined with clearer content hierarchy, improved spacing, and more defined interface components, establishing the foundation for the final visual design.

Visual Identity

ProjectReady treats client onboarding as a journey through space: a calm, friendly experience that helps nontechnical clients see where they are and what comes next. The identity balances that playful theme with a restrained, premium interface, so the product feels approachable without feeling childish.

Logo — Quiet Luxury

The symbol is a single orbit with one mustard point sitting at 45°, representing progress along a path shown with almost nothing. It pairs with a Manrope Medium wordmark set in caps and tracked wide (+22%) for a quiet, confident tone. Mustard is used only for the point, and the lockup works on light, warm canvas, and deep-space backgrounds.

Color

The visual identity is a field at dusk. Headers fade from a pale sky into deep field green, with mustard reserved for the one main action on each screen. Montserrat keeps text readable on low-end phones, and a single Cormorant Garamond Italic accent adds a human touch. The voice is plain and local, using words like “Walang available na tagahatid sa ngayon” (“No distributor available right now”) instead of error codes. Together, these make prlakal feel warm, trustworthy and built for the farmers it serves.

Each status has a base, a soft background and a text color that passes contrast on that background. Deep-space navy (#1E2530) is reserved for the journey map, so the illustrated moment stands apart from the working UI.

Typography

Four typefaces, each with one job: Manrope for the wordmark, Space Grotesk for headings, DM Sans for body copy, and Space Mono for small labels and milestone markers. The mono labels echo the mission-control feel of the theme while DM Sans keeps reading easy.

Shape and Space

Generous rounded corners (12 to 24px), soft low shadows and a 4/8-based spacing scale give the interface a gentle, tactile feel. Layouts sit on a 12-column desktop grid that adapts to tablet and a single-column mobile layout.

Iconography

Rounded 24px line icons with 2px strokes, recolored only with text or status colors and never filled, so they stay light next to the type.

Illustration

Illustrations are flat, rounded and outline-free. A single astronaut character is the guide throughout, with a big tilted helmet and wide visor. The signature piece is the milestone map: four stops (Project Brief, Documents, Final Approval, Ready for kickoff!) connected by a mustard trajectory across a starfield, with a vermilion pin showing the client exactly where they are.

Final Designs

The final design brings ProjectReady's guided onboarding experience to life through a milestone dashboard and a centralized workspace for managing tasks, project briefs, documents, and approvals. Clear progress indicators, intuitive navigation, and flexible task progression help clients understand their responsibilities, monitor submissions, and complete onboarding confidently without unnecessary interruptions.

Next Steps

The next phase would involve usability testing with direct clients and client representatives to evaluate whether the illustrated journey improves navigation, task comprehension, and progress visibility. Particular attention would be given to how users interpret milestone statuses, handle revision requests, and navigate the experience across different devices. Feedback from these tests would inform refinements to the interface, onboarding instructions, and overall user experience before development.
