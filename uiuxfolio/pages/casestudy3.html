Webfolio –
Personal Portfolio Website

Webfolio is a personal website I designed and developed to showcase my skills, services, and experience as a freelance web designer and developer. It features a homepage, about section, service details, and a contact page—aimed at helping potential clients learn more about my work and easily book appointments.

Platform
Responsive Website
Service
UI/UX Design, Develop
Tools
Figma, Wordpress
Timeline
2 Weeks

Problem Statement

As a freelance web designer and developer, having a centralized platform to showcase skills, experience, and service offerings was essential. Depending only on third-party platforms made it challenging for potential clients to fully grasp the brand and directly book services.

Goal

To design and develop a personal portfolio website that allows potential clients to explore my work, learn about my services, and easily book appointments or reach out.

Impact

The website streamlines my client onboarding process and strengthens my online presence. It acts as my professional digital identity, helping visitors turn into leads.

Final Designs

The final UI features a clean, responsive layout with consistent spacing and typography. I used a limited color palette to maintain focus and avoid visual clutter. Components were built with scalability in mind.

Development

The website was developed using WordPress, allowing for flexible customization and efficient content management. Custom layouts were implemented to match the high-fidelity designs, ensuring consistency between design and final output. The platform’s responsiveness and performance were also optimized for a smooth user experience across devices.
