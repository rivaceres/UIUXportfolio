Agrilakal –
from the farm, straight to the buyer

A farmer-first marketplace. It connects Philippine growers, buyers, distributors and the government, from harvest listing to verified delivery and payment. The name joins agrikultura (farming) and kalakal (goods, trade). to collect emails and drive sales through a limited-time discount offer.

Problem Statement

Filipino smallholder farmers often can’t find reliable buyers before harvest. Produce goes unsold or sells at low farmgate prices. Institutional buyers struggle to find supply and coordinate purchases across locations. Price information is uneven, logistics are fragmented, and there is no trusted record of what was agreed, delivered or paid.

How might we give verified farmers earlier visibility to buyers, fair reference-based pricing and coordinated delivery, in a flow simple enough for low-bandwidth phones and six local languages, so more of every harvest is sold and paid for?

Target Users

Primary Users
Farmers want to get verified, list harvests before picking, compare offers and get paid. They need simple step-by-step flows in their own language, low-bandwidth support, and LGU help when needed.
Buyers and wholesalers want to find crops by region, volume and date, and to negotiate and manage deliveries. They need verified sellers, a clear record of agreed terms, and a way to raise quality claims.
Distributors want predictable jobs within their area and capacity, with clear pickup details and a way to report breakdowns.
Secondary Users
DA National and Regional Administrators handle verification, disputes and reference prices.
br> LGU Agriculture Officers run local identity checks and assisted onboarding. Their access is delegated and revocable, never the farmer’s password.

Goal

To design a high-converting lead generation funnel that captures email signups through an irresistible offer and guides users through a seamless purchase journey.

Impact

The funnel allows the brand to grow its email list while simultaneously driving purchases with a limited-time offer. It demonstrates how strategic funnel design supports both conversion and brand trust.

Development

The funnel was developed using Systeme.io, a platform designed for building streamlined marketing workflows. Each page—opt-in, OTO, checkout, and thank-you—was structured to align with the visual mockups and copy provided. Automation features, tagging, email sequences, and PayPal integration were set up to support a smooth lead capture and conversion process.
