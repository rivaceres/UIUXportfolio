Webfolio – Tech Portfolio

A website I designed and developed to highlight my expertise as a software engineer and UI/UX designer. It showcases my projects, skills, and career journey— helping potential clients and employers see how I can bring clarity, creativity, and technical precision to their ideas.

Platform
Responsive Website
Service
UI/UX Design, Develop
Tools
Figma, Angular, Bootstrap
Timeline
1.5 Weeks

Problem Statement

Potential clients and employers often struggled to get a complete picture of my skills and process through static resumes or scattered project links. I needed a centralized, engaging platform that clearly demonstrated both my technical capabilities and design thinking.

Goal

To design and develop a responsive, user-centered portfolio website that highlights my career journey, showcases my projects, and makes it effortless for potential clients or employers to reach out or download my CV.

Impact

The project elevated my professional visibility and credibility, turning my portfolio into an active tool for connection, collaboration, and opportunity.

Process

The project began with establishing a clear information architecture to define the structure and flow of content across the platform. This helped outline key sections and ensure intuitive navigation.

Final Designs

The final UI features a clean, responsive layout with consistent spacing and typography. I used a limited color palette to maintain focus and avoid visual clutter. Components were built with scalability in mind.

Development

The website was developed using Angular, as the core framework, leveraging HTML, CSS, and TypeScript to ensure a modular and scalable codebase. I also integrated Bootstrap for a responsive layout system, allowing the design to adapt seamlessly and function smoothly across all screen sizes.
