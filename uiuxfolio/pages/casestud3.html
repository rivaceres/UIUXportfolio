Nourish Nibbles –
Lead Generation Funnel for Premium Dog Treats

A lead generation funnel designed for a premium dog treat brand, created as part of an online class activity. Built using Systeme.io with mockups from Figma, the project includes an opt-in page, one-time offer, checkout, and thank-you page—designed to collect emails and drive sales through a limited-time discount offer.

Platform
Responsive Website
Service
UI/UX Design, Develop
Tools
Figma, Systeme.io
Timeline
1.5 Weeks

Problem Statement

Many premium pet treat brands struggle to collect qualified leads and convert them into paying customers through a smooth, persuasive online experience. There was a need to design a funnel that captures user interest and drives immediate purchases.

Goal

To design a high-converting lead generation funnel that captures email signups through an irresistible offer and guides users through a seamless purchase journey.

Impact

The funnel allows the brand to grow its email list while simultaneously driving purchases with a limited-time offer. It demonstrates how strategic funnel design supports both conversion and brand trust.

Development

The funnel was developed using Systeme.io, a platform designed for building streamlined marketing workflows. Each page—opt-in, OTO, checkout, and thank-you—was structured to align with the visual mockups and copy provided. Automation features, tagging, email sequences, and PayPal integration were set up to support a smooth lead capture and conversion process.
